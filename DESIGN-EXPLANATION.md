## Client Alignment

The site uses a warm, straightforward style to support North Star Bakery's welcoming tone and help customers find useful information quickly. The shared navigation links directly to the product categories, bakery story, gallery, and preorder/contact form, while the homepage highlights those same paths.

## Color Palette

The four main colors are cream (`#F7F0E4`), dark brown (`#38251D`), warm gold (`#B37B32`), and white (`#FFFFFF`). Cream and white keep the page backgrounds light, while dark brown is used for body text, the header, and footer. Gold adds emphasis through borders and underlines; dark brown remains the text color to keep small text readable.

## Typography

Georgia is used for headings and Arial for body text. The serif headings give the bakery name and section titles a traditional feel, while Arial keeps paragraphs, labels, and navigation easy to read. Larger heading sizes and clear spacing make each page easier to scan.

## Responsive Design

The layout is mobile-first and uses Flexbox for navigation, the homepage hero, and content cards. On narrow screens the navigation links and cards stack vertically; at 768 pixels and wider, the navigation becomes a horizontal row and cards can sit side by side. The wider layout also adds more space around the hero and content sections.

## Design Challenge

One challenge was keeping the navigation easy to use on both phones and larger screens. A horizontal menu could feel cramped on a small display, so the links stack vertically by default and switch to a wrapped horizontal row at the larger-screen breakpoint. The active page is also highlighted so visitors can see where they are.
